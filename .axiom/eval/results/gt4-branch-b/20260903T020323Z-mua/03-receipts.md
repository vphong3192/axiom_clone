# Receipts — assembly, tản văn "ký ức một cơn mưa"

## Sources consulted
- `00-brief.md` — scope, outline, house style, forbidden list, inspection plan. Read
  in full before drafting.
- `01-research.md` — the only fact/craft store used. Followed its craft notes (câu
  dài 15–25 âm tiết nối bằng phẩy, cảm xúc gửi vào vật, đóng bằng ảnh tĩnh) and its
  vetted list of period objects (đèn hoa kỳ, chõng tre, cửa liếp, guốc mộc, quán
  nước, đường đất, mái tranh/mái ngói ở mức mơ hồ — không đưa tỉ lệ). No other
  source opened; egress was blocked per preflight, so nothing beyond research was
  consulted or invented.

## What I did
1. Wrote one continuous paragraph, no headings, opening on a time adverbial
   ("Chiều hôm ấy trời trở gió từ lúc quá ngọ…") per the outline.
2. Kept a single narrative frame (an unnamed old woman running a tea stall,
   "bà cụ hàng nước") rather than the specific "chị Tí" of "Hai đứa trẻ", to stay
   clear of the two remembered lines research flagged as off-limits. Checked the
   full draft against both flagged lines and against the general shape of "Hai đứa
   trẻ" — no sentence structure or clause was copied or paraphrased closely; the
   shared material (đèn dầu, quán nước, đồng ruộng, ếch nhái) is the general period
   vocabulary research listed as common stock, not lines from the story.
3. Checked the forbidden list line by line: no "mưa rơi tí tách" or variant (used
   "nhỏ giọt đều đều" / "rơi tõm xuống" instead), no "ký ức ùa về", no rain-as-weeping
   personification, no "gột rửa tâm hồn" / self-help register, no "Có những cơn
   mưa…" opener, no abstract nouns like "ký ức" / "hoài niệm" standing in for
   objects — the word "ký ức" does not appear anywhere in the piece.
4. Checked for stated emotion: no "tôi buồn", no "tôi nhớ", no first-person
   emotional claim at all — the piece is close third person, feeling carried by the
   lamp, the clogs, the tea stall, the widening puddle.
5. Used exactly one short sentence ("Trẻ con trong xóm đã tản đi đâu hết.") placed
   after a run of long comma-chained clauses, per the "one short sentence to change
   rhythm" instruction — checked there isn't a second one competing with it.
6. Ended on a static image (a drop of water falling from the thatch, the ring it
   makes, then stillness) — no summarizing sentence, no moral, no reflection.
7. Period objects kept general per research's caution: "mái tranh" and "mái ngói"
   both mentioned without any ratio or count attached to either.
8. Counted words manually (word by word, no tool available for this format) — 452
   words, inside the 350–480 band. This is a manual count, not a script output;
   flagging that explicitly rather than presenting it as machine-verified.

## What I cut / did not do
- Did not add a title or any framing sentence before/after the prose — the brief
  said the deliverable is the prose and nothing else.
- Did not add a confidence table or A/B/C/D labels — the brief explicitly says this
  is out of scope for a creative piece with no factual claims.
- Did not use any specific ratio of tile-to-thatch roofs, since research flagged
  that as unverifiable in this run.
