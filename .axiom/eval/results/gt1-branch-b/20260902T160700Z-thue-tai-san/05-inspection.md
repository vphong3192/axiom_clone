## Minors (not blocking — none of these enter the verdict)

1. **The opening thesis has no table row.** "Việt Nam chưa có thuế tài sản thường niên đúng
   nghĩa như ở OECD" (line 5) is the framing claim of the whole piece and carries neither an
   inline tag nor its own row. It is supported by `01-research.md` key finding 1 [B], it is
   covered by the blanket "tất cả dừng ở B" at line 3, and row 1 of the table (the nearest
   tax hits land only, not houses) substantiates it — so it is not unsupported. A dedicated
   row would close the loop.
2. **"Tỷ trọng thu từ đất" is a slightly wider label than the fact store carries.** Research
   attributes 6,6% / 14,7% / 15–16% specifically to *tiền sử dụng đất*; line 13 labels them
   "thu từ đất" (all land-related revenue). The direction of the looseness is *understating*
   the aggregate, the surrounding sentence is about one-off allocation revenue, the table row
   preserves the ambiguity ("Tỷ trọng tiền sử dụng đất/thu từ đất"), and nothing downstream
   rests on the number — hence minor. Writing "tiền sử dụng đất" in the prose too would remove
   the last hairline of imprecision. The "15–16% gần đây" item also inherits its denominator
   (ngân sách địa phương) from the previous clause rather than stating it.
3. **One lexicon slip.** Line 15 renders Đặng Hùng Võ's view as "thuế bất động sản Việt Nam
   hiện quá thấp"; research's wording is "thuế **liên quan** bất động sản". With the 0,03%
   figure attached the referent is unambiguous, and line 5 has already established that no
   property tax exists, so there is no contradiction — but restoring "liên quan" would keep
   the locked lexicon perfectly clean.
4. **Citation-tag placement on the HoREA sentence.** The tag "[B, HoREA qua baomoi.com]"
   sits at the end of a sentence whose final clause ("hạ tầng định giá công bằng hiện chưa
   tồn tại") is the writer's inference — research marks it with "nghĩa là". The inference is
   tight (HoREA's own charge is that the pricing mechanism chases the market instead of
   reflecting value, and the parcel-level price database is only now being commissioned), it
   is graded B, which is the correct grade for an inference. Compare line 15, where the tag
   is correctly placed *before* the em-dash so the inference sits outside it — doing the same
   here would be consistent.
5. **Bare `[B]` on the pass-through sentence** while the table names two sources. Reads at a
   glance like a third unattributed argument, which would undercut the "hai lập luận" count
   in the next paragraph. Naming the sources inline fixes it.
6. **Some numbers are sourced only in the table**, not inline (0,03–0,15%; 700 triệu; 0,4%),
   while neighbouring numbers carry inline tags. Nothing is unsourced, but the inline
   discipline is uneven against the house style's "mọi số kèm nguồn".
7. **The lead pro-side premise is unattributed but unmarked** — "khoản thu một lần từ giao
   đất … vốn cạn dần khi quỹ đất công hết" has no source and no grade, while the opposition's
   two unattributed arguments are explicitly flagged. It reads as framing rather than as a
   factual claim, but the asymmetry is visible.
8. **"Ba đề xuất, ba thời điểm"** — only two dates are actually given (2018, ~10/2026); the
   third is a status ("chưa có dự thảo"), not a date.
9. **The ~90% ownership figure sits under "Phía ủng hộ"** but the sentence it supports
   ("chạm tới đa số hộ gia đình chứ không chỉ nhóm giàu") is closer to a caution than to a
   pro argument. Accurate as written; arguably placed in the wrong movement.
10. **Environment limit, not a defect:** with WebFetch blocked, neither the maker nor I could

    itself snippet-derived. It traces correctly to `01-research.md`. Any finer breakdown of
    how the rates step would need a live source and cannot be settled in this environment —
    flagging the truth-ceiling, not alleging an error.
