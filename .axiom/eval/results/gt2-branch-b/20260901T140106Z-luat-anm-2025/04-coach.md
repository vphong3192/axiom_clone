# Coach pass — self-review of 03-deliverable.md

## Clarity
The two dates that the topic most wants merged (10/12/2025 vs. 1/7/2026) are stated
as separate facts in the first two sentences, with the reason they're easy to
conflate spelled out immediately, not buried. The three sources of controversy are
each opened with "Thứ nhất / Thứ hai / Thứ ba" and each names its subject before its
claim — no floating "critics say." One place that could still confuse a fast reader:
the 24h/6h takedown window and the 24h/3h user-data window are both introduced with
"24 giờ" as the anchor number, which is exactly the confusion research flagged. I
kept both in the same paragraph on purpose (so the reader sees the collision rather
than meeting the two figures in separate articles months apart) and added an explicit
"chưa rõ đây là hai nghĩa vụ tách biệt hay một quy định diễn đạt hai cách" sentence
right after — verdict: acceptable, the sentence does the disambiguating work.

## Detail
Every numeric claim (434/443, 91.75%, 24h/6h, 45 vs. 58 articles, 26/6/2025, 1/1/2026,
"sáu tháng" gap between the two laws) carries a date or a named source in the
confidence table. The one place I deliberately compressed a specific figure — "7–10
years" the two old laws ran unmerged, cut to "nhiều năm" during the final word-count
trim — is noted in the receipts as a defensible compression, not silently dropped.

## Completeness
All five outline movements are present at roughly their assigned weight, plus the
confidence table. The three contested threads (business/VCCI, centralization,
free-expression/HRW) and the "bundled legislation" commentary aside are all covered.
Nothing outside the agreed scope (one law, its changes, who is contesting it and on
what official grounds) was added — the Personal Data Protection Law and Decree 147
appear only where needed to keep the four instruments distinct, per the brief's own
scope note.

## Alternatives
A stronger version of this piece would exist if egress were not blocked and I could
resolve the 45-vs-58-article count and the 24h/3h-vs-24h/6h question by opening a
primary text. I did not manufacture a resolution to either — both stay flagged as
open in the body and the table, which is the honest choice given a B-grade ceiling,
but it does mean the piece has two loose threads a reader might want tied off. That's
a real limitation of the piece, not a framing choice, and it's stated as such.

## Honesty
Checked line by line: no claim reads as more certain than "B" — the opening paragraph
states the ceiling in prose, not only in the table footer. The HRW paragraph was the
highest-risk sentence in the whole piece; re-read after every trim (it survived two
rounds of cuts) to confirm it never says HRW criticized the 2025 law, only that some
summaries make that leap and this piece does not. No number is stated as a
conclusion without its date and source attached in the same sentence or the adjoining
table row.

## Steelman
The "tập trung thẩm quyền" (centralization) paragraph is the thinnest of the three
controversy threads — it has no named critic, just "một số bình luận." That is
honest (research did not surface a named critic for this specific point, only the
observation itself), but it is also the weakest argument in the piece as delivered.
I chose not to inflate it with a name research didn't support, per Axiom 6 — the
alternative (leaving it out) would have understated a real axis of the debate that
appeared consistently in commentary sources, so keeping it thin-but-honest is the
better of the two available options.

## Wow
The piece's genuine value-add over a generic explainer is structural, not
rhetorical: naming the actor and date for every claim in the "who is objecting"
section, so a reader walks away knowing not just what the controversy is but which
specific claims about it are solid (VCCI's own words, HRW's own words) versus which
are the "everyone knows" attributions (HRW "condemning" the 2025 law, AmCham/EuroCham
"objecting") that don't survive a source check. That is the actual finding of this
research pass, and the piece is built to make it visible rather than smoothing over it.
