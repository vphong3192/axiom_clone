### INTERNAL CONTRADICTION — §4, the "12 ngày" interval: FIX-IT
- §4 reads: "Mỹ rút khỏi JETP ngày **4/3/2025** … nhưng Việt Nam vẫn phê duyệt PDP8 điều
  chỉnh **chỉ 12 ngày sau đó (15/4/2025)**."
- The two dates in the same sentence contradict the stated interval. 4/3/2025 (4 March) →
  15/4/2025 (15 April) = **42 days**, not 12. This is a self-contradiction on a number,
  and the number is load-bearing: the "chỉ 12 ngày" is doing the rhetorical work of the
  causal point (VN pressed ahead almost immediately after the US pulled out, therefore US
  pressure was not decisive). Both dates match 01-research.md (US withdrawal 4/3/2025; QĐ
  768 15/4/2025), so the dates are the anchor and the "12 ngày" interval is the error.
- Same claim recurs in §9 ("Việt Nam vẫn xúc tiến PDP8 điều chỉnh ngay sau khi Mỹ rút")
  — §9's looser "ngay sau khi" is fine and survives; only §4's specific "12 ngày" is
  internally false and must be reconciled.
- Required fix: correct the interval to the true span (~6 tuần / ~42 ngày), or replace the
  hard count with the qualitative "khoảng sáu tuần sau" / "chỉ hơn một tháng sau." Do not
  ship "12 ngày" with the dates 4/3 and 15/4 standing beside it. The causal argument itself
  is unharmed by the correction (VN still approved PDP8 after the US exit); only the
  false specificity needs removing.
